DERMAI REPORT - 6-PAGE TRIMMING SUGGESTIONS
=============================================
Compare: egpaper_for_review.tex (original, 8 body pages)
     vs: egpaper_for_review_v2.tex (trimmed, 6 body pages)

Body page count = Intro through Broader Impact, i.e. everything EXCLUDING
Team Contributions, References, and Appendix. The instructor has confirmed
the Appendix is exempt from the 6-page limit, same as Team Contributions
and References (only those two are stated as exempt in the official
CS7643 project guidelines; Appendix exemption was separately confirmed
by the teacher).

Verified by compiling both files with pdflatex:
  - egpaper_for_review.tex    -> 8 pages of body content
  - egpaper_for_review_v2.tex -> 6 pages of body content (confirmed)

Nothing was deleted outright except duplicate/placeholder text (see below).
All data, tables, and figures from the original are still in v2 -- either
in the main body or moved to the Appendix with a pointer from the main text.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT CHANGED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. TWO TABLES CONDENSED IN PLACE, FULL VERSIONS MOVED TO APPENDIX
   - Table 1 (Strategy 1 vs. Strategy 2 fine-tuning depth sweep): the
     original showed all 4 depths x 2 strategies x 2 architectures
     (~15 numbers in a two-part full-width table). v2's main-text version
     shows only the best result per strategy per architecture (4 numbers,
     single-column table). The full sweep now lives in Appendix A.1,
     referenced from the main text.
   - Table 3 (weighted vs. unweighted loss): the original reported 8
     columns (train/val/test x avg/best) x 4 rows. v2's main-text version
     keeps only Test-mF1 and Test-acc (the numbers actually used to justify
     the final decision), 4 rows. Full breakdown moved to Appendix A.3.
   - The hyperparameter search grid (7 bulleted lists of swept values) was
     condensed to one sentence in the main text; the full grid moved to
     Appendix A.2.
   This is the single biggest space saving and required the confirmed
   Appendix exemption -- without it, this move doesn't help.

2. DELETED ALL \todo{} PLACEHOLDER BLOCKS (5 total)
   These were internal team notes, not reader-facing content (e.g. "per
   Sebastian, 7/16", "regenerate these curves..."). We checked: every
   substantive caveat inside them (single-seed vs. planned 3-seed,
   the unresolved insertion-AUC anomaly, the checkpoint mismatch between
   faithfulness and localization) is already stated in proper prose in the
   Future Work section, so nothing was lost -- this was pure duplication.
   Removing them also directly helps the "is the manuscript self-contained"
   clarity criterion in the rubric (Additional, 5 pts).

3. BROADER IMPACT: replaced a \todo placeholder (zero real content) with
   2 real sentences using the actual substance that was inside the todo
   note. This isn't a rubric-required section, so it was also folded into
   the end of Future Work as a labeled paragraph ("Broader impact:") rather
   than its own subsection, to save the heading's vertical space.

4. CUT REDUNDANT RESTATEMENT OF THE SAME NUMBERS
   The pair (test macro-F1 0.773 vs. 0.696), (deletion AUC 0.174 vs 0.646),
   and the IoU/PG numbers originally appeared 3-4 times each across
   Abstract, Results-summary, Discussion, and Conclusion. v2 keeps them in
   Abstract + Discussion/Conclusion (where the interpretation happens) and
   refers back to the section elsewhere instead of re-quoting digits.

5. CONDENSED PROSE THROUGHOUT (no content dropped, just tightened)
   - Section 3.1.1 (Fine-Tuning Strategy Selection): merged the
     Strategy-1/Strategy-2 description and shortened the results paragraph
     by about half.
   - Section 3.1.2 (Overfitting Mitigation): tightened the per-architecture
     findings paragraph and the "architecture-dependent asymmetry"
     paragraph.
   - Approach (LP-FT mechanism paragraph): kept the causal explanation
     (why training the head first matters) but cut the step-by-step
     narrative restating the same mechanism a second time.
   - Discussion subsections (Problem Structure, Model Components, I/O
     Representations, Loss Function and Regularization): each tightened
     20-30%. NOTE: these map directly to named rubric bullets ("what parts
     had learned parameters," "what representations," "loss function,"
     "framework/existing code"), so they were condensed, not deleted --
     the rubric explicitly allows "only a brief mention" for standard
     items.
   - Merged "Transfer Learning Impact" and "Framework and Existing Code"
     into one subsection ("Transfer Learning and Implementation") since
     the latter mostly repeated the Approach-section footnote.
   - Future Work: both paragraphs (immediate items / extensions)
     tightened by ~25-35%, content preserved.
   - Conclusion: removed one duplicate set of parenthetical numbers
     (already stated in Discussion immediately before it).
